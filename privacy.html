Privacy.

Hillside collects only what you give us: an email address if you join our list, and the details a ticketing platform shares with us when you buy a ticket — name, email, what you bought. We use them to send event updates, receipts, and thank-yous. We do not sell or rent them, and we do not share them with anyone except the services that run our website, tickets, and books.

Our website does not use advertising trackers. Our finance tools connect to our own bank account, read-only, to keep our books; no donor payment details are stored by Hillside.

To see, correct, or delete what we hold about you, email events@hillsidenewyork.com.
