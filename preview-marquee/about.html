Behind Hillside.

“The name is a bridge — between the Hilltop where the three of us first met and Riverside where the Gwozdz family grew up.”

Hillside began in Tyler's memory, and it runs on two convictions: a community worth belonging to, and real support for the organizations confronting addiction in America.

The first gathering was a black-tie gala at the Bowery Hotel. The next is to be announced.
